## How does Agentrixx work with clients?

Our engineering process follows four clear stages:

1. **Discover** — Understand the business problem, users, goals, and constraints.  
2. **Design** — Define the product approach, architecture, and user experience.  
3. **Build** — Develop, test, and deliver the solution.  
4. **Improve** — Monitor performance, gather feedback, and continuously enhance the product.  

## What technologies does Agentrixx use?

Our technology stack includes **Next.js, React, TypeScript, Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, AWS, and OpenAI technologies**. The specific tools selected depend on the project’s requirements, scale, security needs, and long-term goals.

## Selected Projects

### Ubuntu Voice

A privacy-first, low-bandwidth RAG platform designed for conflict-affected communities. It supports GPS incident recording, map-based visibility, and community-defined AI agents across web chat, WhatsApp, SMS, and private email workflows.

### Customer Support SaaS

A multi-tenant AI support platform where authenticated users can create companies, upload PDF knowledge bases, search content using vector search, and chat with tenant-specific support assistants. Built with Next.js and FastAPI.

### HomeHunt Kenya

A property discovery platform that helps people find apartments, houses, rooms, and other rental homes in Kenya. Built with Next.js and TypeScript, with a focus on a clear and user-friendly experience.

### Payment Manager

A rental-management system for landlords and tenants. It includes registration, login, JWT authentication, role-based access, and separate dashboards for each user type. Built with Next.js and FastAPI.

### Material Monitoring System

A materials-tracking platform for an oil manufacturing company. Built with Next.js, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL to support reliable record-keeping and operational oversight.
